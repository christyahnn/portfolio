TEC-01 ECON 122 · Final Project

Flight Tracking Analysis

Dataset
10,000 U.S. commercial flights (2024 sample)
Method
EDA, gradient boosting, PCA, linear regression
Outcome
Identified operational (not weather) as top delay driver

Question. Where, when, and why do U.S. flights get delayed or cancelled — and can that be predicted?

Method. Explored delay/cancellation patterns by carrier, route, time, and geography, then modeled delay occurrence (boosting, PCA) and delay length (linear regression) separately once class imbalance limited the first approach.

Finding. Late-aircraft and carrier-controlled delays outweighed weather as root causes; delays concentrated at major hubs (DFW, ATL, DEN) and in the Eastern U.S., with peak season adding 6.3 minutes of delay on average versus the shoulder season.

TEC-02 Independent Project · R

Credit Risk Analysis

Dataset
5,000+ loan applications (training + test)
Method
Logistic regression, random forest, gradient boosting
Outcome
0.88 AUC (acceptance); $1.34M expected profit on test loans

Question. Which borrowers should be approved, at what interest rate, to maximize profit while managing default risk?

Method. Engineered four risk-relevant features, then compared four supervised models by cross-validated AUC before selecting gradient boosting as the final model.

Finding. Boosting reached 0.88 AUC on acceptance and 0.72 on default — used to set a per-borrower profit-maximizing interest rate and approve/deny policy, producing $1.34M in expected profit across 1,000 test loans.
