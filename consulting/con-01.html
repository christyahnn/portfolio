The Ask

A clean-ingredient, functional-snack startup preparing to launch publicly engaged our team to build an optimal go-to-market plan. Throughout the project, we focused on marketing, pricing, and positioning — grounded in real consumer data rather than internal assumptions.

Approach

Market analysis. Segmented target consumers into college students, working professionals, and parents of school-aged children; benchmarked four direct competitors; and sized the serviceable obtainable market using a bottom-up model.

Consumer testing. Designed and fielded a 55-response survey with university-aged consumers to test packaging and flavor preferences directly, rather than relying on secondary research alone.

Strategy design. Built a phased marketing plan split into short-term moves — a social media overhaul, a campus ambassador program, and brand transparency initiatives — and longer-term plays, including mobile sampling activations, an expert-backed content series, and a loyalty program.

Key Findings

  • Minimalist, low-waste packaging was the single most requested feature (39 of 55 mentions), ahead of resealable pouches and travel-friendly formats.
  • Estimated first-year market capture of $14.8K–$73.9K, based on a conservative 0.01%–0.05% share of a $147.7M serviceable obtainable market.
  • 66% of healthy snackers are aged 18–44, and 64% are willing to pay a premium for organic ingredients — both findings directly shaped the packaging and pricing recommendations.

Recommendation

A two-horizon marketing roadmap: rebuild the brand's digital presence and campus reach in the short term, while building durable engagement mechanisms — sampling, content, and loyalty — over the following years.

My Role

Served as project lead for this client engagement — the main point of communication between the client and the team, and responsible for organizing the semester-long workflow and plan. Conducted research, analysis, and visualization work alongside the team, including consumer survey design, competitor and market-sizing research, and the final recommendations.
