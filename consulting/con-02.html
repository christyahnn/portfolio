Growth Strategy: Reviving a Legacy Beverage Brand

Case Study Competition · 2025

The Prompt

A craft kombucha company was facing declining revenue driven by a stagnant product line, outdated marketing, and distribution that didn't match where its target consumers actually shopped. Our team had a fixed 48-hour window to diagnose the problem and build a five-year growth strategy.

Approach

Diagnose root causes. No product innovation in five years, underrepresentation in grocery stores (55% of category sales), and a brand narrative indistinguishable from competitors.

Build a franchise-based expansion strategy. Targeted Asian American communities and Millennial/Gen Z consumers on the U.S. West Coast, organized around three levers: flavor innovation, channel realignment, and modern marketing. The strategy was grounded in research into kombucha's historical origins in East Asia and its cultural heritage around fermented foods — paying homage to the beverage's roots while driving authentic engagement.

Size the opportunity. Used a bottom-up TAM → SAM → SOM model anchored to the 2024 craft kombucha market, then built a year-by-year revenue plan to defend the recommendation's feasibility.

Key Findings

  • The craft kombucha category held roughly $2.48B in total addressable market, with a $955M serviceable segment among Gen Z and Millennial consumers.
  • Herbal and fruity flavor lines were projected to grow at 8.2% and 4.1% CAGR respectively — informing the top product recommendation.
  • A grocery-first distribution strategy directly matched where the target demographic already shopped, rather than requiring new consumer behavior.

Recommendation

A five-year plan moving from $198M to a $250M revenue target, sequenced from flavor R&D and grocery outreach in Year 1 through full franchise expansion by Year 5.

My Role

Navigated large, unstructured datasets — filtering out noise to clean, analyze, and synthesize raw data and graphs into actionable business insights. Contributed directly to market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), financial modeling, and channel/marketing strategy to deliver the final presentation within 48 hours of topic release.
