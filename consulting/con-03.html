CON-03

User Testing a New Feature

Pomona Consulting Group · Client Engagement · 2026

3.3/5 Enjoyability How much users enjoyed using the feature, confirming the concept landed well on its own terms.
1.79/5 Vs. existing formats How the feature compared to content formats users already relied on. This is where the gap showed up.

The Ask

A nonprofit knowledge-sharing platform was piloting a new content-creation feature and needed an honest read on whether it was ready for broader release — with validation from real users rather than internal assumptions.

Approach

Moderated user testing. Collected data on ease of use, enjoyability, topical interest, and — most critically — how the new feature compared to content formats users already relied on.

Synthesized qualitative and quantitative responses. Paired 1–5 engagement scores with verbatim user quotes to explain why the numbers looked the way they did, not just what they were.

Translated findings into a prioritized product roadmap. Used the collected data to uncover insights and convey them in a simple, easy-to-understand deck for the client.

Key Findings

  • Users rated the feature highly on ease of creation (3.9/5) and enjoyability (3.3/5) — the underlying concept worked.
  • But engagement compared to existing formats scored just 1.79 out of 5, and 77.8% of users said they'd use the feature occasionally or not at all.
  • The gap wasn't a lack of interest, but a missing personalization layer: users had no way to filter content to topics they actually cared about, which surfaced repeatedly in session feedback.

Recommendation

Four prioritized product bets, ranked by expected impact: (1) interest-based personalization and discovery filters, (2) an expanded content-creation toolkit, (3) gamification to build a return habit, and (4), as a longer-term bet, exploring a different content format altogether — some users indicated the underlying idea might land better outside its current shape.

My Role

Conducted the research, analysis, and visualization work alongside a partner, including the research design, moderated user testing sessions, and the final client-facing recommendation deck.
