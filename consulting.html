CON-01 Pomona Consulting Group · 2025

Go-to-Market Strategy for a Pre-Launch Snack Brand

Client / Context
Clean-ingredient, functional snack startup pre-launch
Role
Project lead — client point of contact, workflow, analysis
Outcome
Two-horizon marketing roadmap; $14.8K–$73.9K Y1 capture

Situation. A clean-ingredient, functional snack startup preparing to launch publicly needed a go-to-market plan grounded in real consumer data, not assumptions.

Approach. Segmented target consumers, benchmarked four direct competitors, sized the market bottom-up, and fielded a 55-response survey to test packaging and flavor preferences directly.

Result. Delivered a phased marketing roadmap — short-term brand and campus moves, long-term sampling and loyalty plays — with packaging and pricing recommendations backed by the survey data.

CON-02 Case Study Competition · 2025

Growth Strategy: Reviving a Legacy Beverage Brand

Client / Context
Craft kombucha company facing declining revenue
Role
Market sizing, financial modeling, channel strategy
Outcome
5-year plan targeting $198M → $250M in revenue

Situation. A craft kombucha company was losing ground to a stagnant product line, outdated marketing, and distribution that didn't match where its target consumers actually shopped — with a fixed 48-hour window to diagnose and recommend.

Approach. Diagnosed root causes, then built a franchise-based expansion strategy around three levers — flavor innovation, channel realignment, and modern marketing — sized with a bottom-up TAM → SAM → SOM model.

Result. A five-year growth plan moving from $198M to a $250M revenue target, sequenced from flavor R&D and grocery outreach in Year 1 through full franchise expansion by Year 5.

CON-03 Pomona Consulting Group · 2026

User Testing a New Feature

Client / Context
Nonprofit knowledge-sharing platform, new feature pilot
Role
Research design, moderated testing, recommendation deck
Outcome
Four prioritized product bets, ranked by impact

Situation. A nonprofit knowledge-sharing platform was piloting a new content-creation feature and needed an honest, user-validated read on whether it was ready for broader release.

Approach. Ran moderated user testing sessions, pairing 1–5 engagement scores with verbatim quotes to explain why the numbers looked the way they did — not just what they were.

Result. Found the concept worked (3.9/5 ease of creation) but engagement lagged existing formats (1.79/5) due to a missing personalization layer — leading to four ranked product recommendations.
